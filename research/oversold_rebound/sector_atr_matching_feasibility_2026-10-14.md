# 2026-10-14 sector/ATR matching feasibility audit (40 named stocks)

NO TRADE. This is a **matching feasibility audit**, NOT a valid matched-control performance estimate. The universe was assembled from known names after October 15 outcomes were visible; therefore retrospective selection bias remains. Webull daily through October 13 and RTH M5 as-of Oct 14 15:00 ET; outcome Oct 15 15:45 ET. Prior panel had 20 oversold candidates. Additional 20 industry-relevant symbols: COIN IBKR SCHW MSTR MARA RIOT HUT WDC STX SNDK INTC QCOM AVGO TSM ASML TER ONTO SMCI HPE CRWD. All 40 returned sufficient daily and M5 data. Only the 20 prior oversold names constitute the frozen treatment panel; additions are a convenience peer search, not an exhaustive nonselloff universe.

Pre-outcome ATR14 percentage examples (14 prior completed daily bars): HOOD 4.22%; MU 4.63%; COIN 5.13%; IBKR 2.67%; SCHW 1.79%; QCOM 3.79%; TER 5.01%; AVGO 2.90%; TSM 2.48%; ASML 2.85%; HPE 5.32%; CRWD 4.01%; MSTR 5.35%; INTC 5.37%; ONTO 5.66%.

Outcome examples from Oct 14 15:00 to Oct 15 15:45: HOOD +2.92%, COIN +7.52%, IBKR +1.86%, SCHW -2.05%; MU +0.40%, QCOM +3.73%, TER -4.22%, AVGO -0.12%, TSM -1.54%, ASML -1.44%, INTC -1.86%, ONTO -3.66%. **Do not interpret any of these as valid matched-control differences.** For HOOD, COIN has ~21.6% relative ATR distance (5.13 vs 4.22) but COIN's 5-session decline was not checked using an as-of 15:00 close, so nonselloff eligibility is not certified. For MU, TER has ~8.2% ATR distance (5.01 vs 4.63) but a distinct semiconductor equipment business and possible contemporaneous selloff; nonselloff eligibility not certified. Other peers exceed the suggested 20% caliper or are different industries.

Methodological issue: prior reports used arbitrary panel selections and price-return endpoints, not actual completed-bar entry trigger, stop-first outcome, or realistic options execution. The 40-stock pool does not solve sampling bias. A truly valid test must (1) obtain a complete dated point-in-time stock universe with sector/subindustry labels and survivorship handling; (2) lock treatment eligibility and match caliper (proposed ATR percent relative gap <=20%, price/liquidity filters, same subindustry then industry then sector with strata reported separately) *before* seeing outcomes; (3) select controls with no qualifying selloff and with comparable prior 5-day return or explicitly account for that structural difference; (4) mark unmatched cases rather than forcing bad controls; (5) compare next-session paths and controls using timestamped bars and actual executable rules. No thresholds changed; 283-event negative audit remains the strongest broad evidence.
