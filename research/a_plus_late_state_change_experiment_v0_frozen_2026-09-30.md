# A+ LATE STATE-CHANGE EXPERIMENT v0 — FROZEN RESEARCH SPEC

Frozen: 2026-09-30 after the regular-session close.
Status: RESEARCH ONLY. This does NOT modify A+ DAY DETECTOR v1.0 and does NOT grant live trade permission.

## Why this exists
Prospective A+ v1.0 scorekeeping has repeatedly produced directionally useful morning candidates that remained WATCH / DEVELOPING because the full executable confirmation + fresh-runway package was not present at 09:50 or 10:20, followed by a later directional move.

Observed persisted examples motivating this experiment include:
- 2026-09-21: WATCH / DEVELOPING -> later strong upside.
- 2026-09-28: WATCH / DEVELOPING -> later downside.
- 2026-09-29: WATCH / DEVELOPING -> later downside.
- 2026-09-30: WATCH / DEVELOPING -> later/morning upside.

These observations motivate a new experiment; they MUST NOT be used to loosen, relabel, or optimize A+ DAY DETECTOR v1.0.

## Frozen hypothesis
Some sessions that remain WATCH / DEVELOPING at 10:20 may later undergo a genuine state change in which the original material-information thesis becomes executable only after additional RTH acceptance develops.

The experiment asks whether a separately specified late checkpoint can identify those transitions with useful precision while preserving v1.0's aggressive abstention discipline.

## Eligibility
A session is eligible for late-state-change research only if ALL are true:
1. The frozen v1.0 final 10:20 state was WATCH / DEVELOPING, not A+ and not retroactively changed.
2. A specific material driver / origin family was already identified prospectively.
3. The original causal transmission remains economically coherent; later price movement alone cannot manufacture a catalyst.
4. The thesis/origin label remains unchanged from the morning record.
5. No unresolved imminent binary event or material distortion veto makes execution non-evaluable.

NO TRADE sessions are not automatically eligible. They may be studied separately but cannot be converted into late A+ observations under this experiment without a future pre-specified rule revision.

## Late checkpoint
Primary experimental checkpoint: 11:00 ET.

Use information available at or before 11:00 only. Do not use afternoon outcomes to determine the 11:00 state.

## Required late-state-change gates — ALL required
1. DRIVER STILL VALID
The prospectively identified material driver remains relevant and has not been contradicted by new information.

2. TRANSMISSION STILL COHERENT
Relevant cross-assets, sector/peer behavior and market interpretation remain directionally coherent. Do not require every asset to agree, but material contradiction in the important economic channel blocks promotion.

3. NEW RTH ACCEPTANCE SINCE 10:20
There must be genuinely new structural evidence after the frozen 10:20 check. Examples include accepted VWAP reclaim/hold for calls or failure/rejection for puts; accepted breakout/breakdown; higher-low/lower-high sequence; failed countertrend repair; persistent relative strength/weakness; stronger directional participation; or acceptance through an important predefined structural level.

A continuation that was already exhausted by 10:20 is not a state change.

4. LEADERSHIP / BREADTH CONFIRMATION
Broad macro setups require credible breadth/leadership participation. Sector/thesis-specific setups may remain narrower only when the vehicle is explicitly confined to that sponsored area.

5. FRESH EXECUTABLE TRIGGER
There must be a specific accepted M5 trigger available at/after the state change. Record the first valid trigger timestamp. No hindsight entry selection.

6. FRESH RUNWAY
At that trigger, a realistic structural destination must still provide approximately >=2:1 current R:R after accounting for range/ADR consumption, nearby Savino/technical destinations where relevant, and first-wave extension. Correct direction with consumed runway remains NO LATE ENTRY.

7. EXECUTION / EVENT VETO
Options liquidity/spread/IV and any scheduled data, Fed event, EIA, expiration, rebalance or abnormal-flow distortion must be acceptable. Quarter-end/rebalance context must be explicitly flagged.

## Experimental outputs
Exactly one 11:00 research state:
- LATE A+ CALL — RESEARCH QUALIFIED
- LATE A+ PUT — RESEARCH QUALIFIED
- STILL WATCH — no qualified state change
- LATE NO TRADE — thesis invalidated, runway consumed, event veto, or other hard failure

These labels are separate from A+ DAY DETECTOR v1.0. Never write them into the v1.0 final-state column.

## Required record for LATE A+
- original 09:20 classification
- original 09:50 classification
- original 10:20 classification
- original family/origin and catalyst
- 11:00 late-state classification
- exact first valid accepted executable trigger and timestamp
- chosen vehicle
- trigger price
- invalidation
- T1 / structural destination
- realistic trigger-time R:R
- relevant VWAP / M5 acceptance evidence
- breadth / leadership evidence
- cross-asset transmission
- event/distortion flags
- RTH MFE from trigger
- RTH MAE from trigger
- close return from trigger
- +0.5%, +0.75%, +1.0% before -0.5%
- T1 vs invalidation first
- directional thesis survived into close: yes/no

## Required record for STILL WATCH / LATE NO TRADE
Record the exact gate(s) preventing qualification and whether abstention:
- avoided a false positive,
- missed a later move,
- or correctly rejected a move with insufficient fresh R:R.

Never retroactively promote based on the afternoon outcome.

## Relationship to Savino / GEX / Discord / other external signals
Savino levels, subdivisions, forecasts, GEX, CROM and Discord signals remain independent research context. They may identify structural destinations or corroboration but cannot by themselves satisfy the material-driver, acceptance, breadth, or execution gates.

Savino/GEX concurrence must be recorded separately so incremental value can later be tested.

## Falsification / promotion criteria
Do not modify thresholds after individual wins/losses. Accumulate a prospective sample first.

Track:
- count of eligible WATCH sessions
- LATE A+ CALL count
- LATE A+ PUT count
- STILL WATCH count
- LATE NO TRADE count
- LATE A+ hit / false-positive rate under the frozen outcome definition
- average and median MFE/MAE
- +0.5/+0.75/+1.0 before -0.5 sequencing
- T1-before-invalidation rate
- incremental capture versus frozen v1.0
- false positives added versus frozen v1.0

Do not surface historical rates as live probabilities.

## Anti-moving-goalpost discipline
- A missed move does not justify weakening a gate.
- A late move does not change the original v1.0 classification.
- The 11:00 state must be reconstructed only from evidence available by 11:00.
- Never select a later trigger because it produced the best outcome.
- Preserve the original catalyst/family label.
- Savino, GEX, Discord and afternoon outcomes cannot manufacture qualification.
- Any future change requires a new version and an untouched prospective/OOS sample.
