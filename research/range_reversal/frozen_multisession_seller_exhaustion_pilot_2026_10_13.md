## Prespecified multi-session causal signal
For each second-test candidate day D (at least 35 prior complete sessions, one complete following session), establish support S from the minimum **morning low** (09:30–12:00) across sessions D-35 through D-16 inclusive. Require at least two morning lows within +1% of S, separated by >=3 trading-session indices; all derived strictly before D-15. Exclude if any prior closing price in D-15..D-1 below 98% of S.

Detect intraday failed support test in 09:30–12:00 ET: first M5 low <=101% of S but >=97% of S; reclaim completed close >=S on that bar or one of next 4 bars; first reclaim completed bar must finish by 12:00 ET. Require previous session close >=98% S. For day D find the most recent earlier qualified morning failed test on a distinct day D-15 through D-1, using SAME S, to compare the last two eligible tests. Take first eligible reclaim on D, place hypothetical share entry at NEXT 5-minute bar's actual OPEN by noon. No lookahead in selection; record if entry not available. For same issuer duplicate qualifying entries within 5 trading sessions, retain first chronologically to avoid overlapping trades.

For both tests, use the lowest observed low from first touch through reclaim (observable at trigger, not subsequent lower lows). Down-pressure proxy = sum(volume on red open-to-close bars)/sum(previous-20-complete-session median volume at matching five-minute times), over six completed bars ending at the observed probe-low bar (inclusive), 09:30-onward, requiring >=2 red bars in EACH test and 20 valid prior time-of-day volume observations for every slot. This is NOT true market sell-order volume. Capture normalization as of EACH test day. Never use future data in denominators.

**Predeclared filters:** Shallower penetration = second probe low at least 0.20% x S higher than first. Diminishing pressure = second relative down-pressure <=85% of first. Both = logical AND. Compare baseline ALL qualified cross-session test2 entries; shallower alone; pressure alone; both; and complement not-both. No threshold optimization in these bars. Also report optional 10:30–noon subset of SAME baseline.
