## Prior established support and retest, no future bars
For each complete candidate session D with >=35 preceding fully observed trading sessions, take DAILY RTH low across 25 sessions D−35..D−11; S = third-lowest daily RTH low (nearest-rank 10th percentile, index2 of ascending 25). Require >=2 distinct sessions among those 25 with daily low within ±1.0% of S, separated by >=3 trading-session indices. Require NO close below 0.98*S in D−10..D−1; preceding D−1 CLOSE >=0.99*S and <=1.05*S. Require continuous verified source coverage.
For any session j in D−10..D, define FIRST morning probe as FIRST five-minute bar with LOW <= S*0.999 (strict undercut >=0.10%) and LOW>=S*0.98 (max 2% below); if first breach low<S*.98 or no completed close >=S in that bar or next TWO completed bars by 12:00 ET, fail the whole session; no scanning for more attractive later tests. Morning bars start 09:30–11:55 (reclaim must finish <=11:55 start). Test is valid if first-breach bar satisfies penetration criterion and close>=S on it or within next two; probe lowest low between initial breach and reclaim <=2% below S; if more severe later undercut between breach/reclaim, invalid. Compare D's SECOND (most recent) valid cross-session probe with last valid FIRST probe among D−10..D−1, using the SAME S. Reclaim time is completed bar END, entry next 5min bar OPEN no later than 12:00 ET; require exact bar timestamps. No additional volume/rejection screen for baseline. Deduplicate: after retained D event, exclude next 5 trading sessions for this stock, irrespective of whether it wins/loses. We do not use any portion of D+1 to identify signal.
## Opening-aware features fixed before outcomes
- First/second undercut depth: (S − minimum observed low from first probe through its reclaim)/S, strictly >=0.10% and <=2.0%.
- Shallower second test: second depth <=0.80*first depth, and decrease >=0.10 percentage POINTS (fractional >=0.001).
- **Probe-bar relative volume** for each test: V at its first undercut 5min bar divided by the MEDIAN of the SAME time slot volumes from the previous20 full RTH sessions preceding THAT test's day. This works for 09:30 bars. This is NOT signed selling volume; **lower volume is a proxy for less activity, not proof selling exhaustion**. Need all20 prior same-slot references; if any missing, pressure feature UNKNOWN and exclude from filter comparisons, but still count valid baseline.
- Lower relative probe volume: second ratio <= 0.80*first ratio. Pair must have BOTH measured.
- Strong reversal candle: on D's initial undercut bar (C−L)/(H−L)>=0.65, if H==L fail; separately report, not required for baseline.
- Both weak depth and relative volume, and complement (not-both among complete measurements). For group comparisons report fractions, not fishing for best threshold.
