# Quality-company earnings selloff recovery: 92-event horizon study

**Frozen:** 2026-10-10. **Status:** exploratory historical cohort, not a validated options strategy.

## Source and cohort

- Fixed 50-stock candidate universe independent of the original seven-name discovery exercise.
- 2023-01-01 through 2026-06-30 announcements (allowing at least 60 subsequent trading sessions to October 9, 2026).
- Historical SEC-linked announcement dates and session labels: https://quant500.com/earnings-date/TICKER (replace TICKER).
- Historical daily regular-session equity OHLC bars, financial statement publication dates, earnings, cash flow, and balance sheet data: connected Webull.
- First qualifying earnings-related selloff: close at least 5% below the pre-announcement stock close on the first or second regular session after the actual announcement (BMO reaction begins same day, AH begins next session).
- Quality as **known before announcement**: positive prior four-quarter net income, positive four-quarter CFO + capex, positive latest comparable quarterly year-over-year revenue growth, latest total debt / equity below 2.0. Financial reports published on or after announcement date are excluded. No interpolation of missing data.
- Raw qualifying declines: **166**. Financial PASS: **92**; FAIL: **69**; insufficient financial data: **5**. An earnings-page search returned usable SEC-linked announcement histories for 47 of the 50 screened stocks.
- 92 price/quality events span **32 symbols**. Current contemporary ticker universe introduces survivorship/selection bias.
- Each result measures the **first qualifying selloff day's closing price** as the baseline. It is **not** a tradable modelled entry price. All price changes refer to the underlying STOCK, not options.

## Adverse excursions

- Median worst subsequent 60-session low vs selloff close: **−8.60%**.
- 67/92 fell at least an additional 5% intraday; 39/92 fell >=10%; 20/92 fell >=20% before the 60-session study ended. These excursions are not path-ordered relative to targets; trade-specific time ordering must be retested for entries.
- Median calendar index of the lowest subsequent intraday low was 25.5 trading sessions. This is a retrospective extreme and is **not** a proposed entry timing trigger.

## Check for contamination from the next earnings announcement

For 91 events, the available SEC-linked history identified the next earnings announcement. In 24 cases, that later announcement occurred before day 60. Truncating each case before the next announcement preserved all 47 full recoveries observed among those 91; none depended on a later quarterly announcement.

The remaining event was PYPL's May 5, 2026 selloff; its next earnings release was July 28, 2026, confirmed by issuer SEC filing and newsroom release. Its full recovery and +5% rebound in this data occurred before that release:
- https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000080/pypl-20260728.htm
- https://newsroom.paypal-corp.com/2026-07-28-PayPal-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results

Thus the 48/92 full-recovery count was **not produced by subsequent quarterly earnings** in the sampled cases. The 60-day closing return itself can still reflect later earnings and other new information; do not attribute all 60-day stock returns solely to the original shock.

## Variation and biases

- Recovery-to-pre-earnings-close within 60 sessions: 2023 11/20; 2024 14/29; 2025 12/29; 2026 11/14. Small and uneven year cohorts.
- Corporate-action adjustments, partial financial restatements, data-feed conventions, and some earnings-publication timestamps may shift exact event classifications.
- The 2026 subset is especially small and calendar-limited. Mean returns can be distorted by large single-stock rebounds.
- This study is not a performance backtest for 7-, 14-, 30-, 45-, 60-, or 90-DTE calls: it has no historical option bid/ask, strike/delta, IV, Greeks, transaction fees, or option fill assumptions.
- Post-earnings-drift research describes extended moves in the **direction of earnings surprise**, including negative drift, so recovery should not be assumed: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214635020303750

## Follow-on frozen test

Compare entry offsets (after 1/3/5/10 sessions, confirmed reversal, first high reclaim), measure risk-adjusted forward stock returns and first target vs drawdown *after the actual entry*, and then evaluate options expiration only with verifiable historical option pricing or clearly labeled option models. Require a new untouched validation sample before operational scanner use.
