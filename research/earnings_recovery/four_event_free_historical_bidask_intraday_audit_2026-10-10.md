# Investing OS — no-cost historical option execution reconstruction, four frozen post-earnings events
Research date: **2026-10-10**. Status: **Retrospective/exploratory; improved historical execution evidence, NOT verified fill-based options P&L or an A+ call signal**.

## Scope and what was actually run
Continued the unchanged four earnings-selloff events and their previously frozen entry dates: DELL 2025-03-03; QCOM 2025-07-31; PYPL 2025-02-04; AMAT 2025-02-14. Entry methods: day-three opening vs first three-rising-lows plus prior-two-high-break signal (enter following opening). Underlying exit: 5% from actual unadjusted stock **entry OPEN**, target/stop with frozen 20 trading-day cap and conservative stop-first ambiguity. Call entry screen: ONE standard 100-share OTM call, >=10 options contracts traded on the entry session, first historical trade debit <=$50 including illustrative $0.65 entry fee; strike closest to spot that satisfies, nominal 30/60 calendar days. Prior original protocol: `affordable_otm_tradebar_pilot_protocol_2026-10-10.md`.

Successfully added TWO free sources, preserving separate data types:

1. **DoltHub** `post-no-preference/options`, branch `master`, unauthenticated SQL: historical **end-of-day** option-chain bids, asks, IV (source column `vol`), delta, gamma, vega, expiry/strike. Actual query retrieval for all 8 entry and corresponding exit dates across four issuers (16 symbol/date/spot-specific requests, 351 returned quote rows counting filtering overlaps). Data preserved in `dolthub_four_event_eod_chain_2026-10-10.json`, reproducible script `run_dolthub_four_event_2026-10-10.py`, and GitHub Action `.github/workflows/earnings-recovery-dolthub-free-2025.yml`. **Source does not include historical DAILY TRADE VOLUME or open interest**, only `vol` = implied volatility. Its 2025 chain snapshots include only around three sampled expiries per date, not the whole listed chain. Many source-listed expiries differ between an entry date and exit date; missing a contract on exit is *not proof it had zero value or was delisted*. EOD asks/bids are NOT synchronized with the frozen next-OPEN entry or intraday stock target. An alternative EOD ask-only selection has no ability to certify the frozen >=10-entry-volume rule.
2. **Massive connected market data** historical **5-minute stock and specific listed option TRADE OHLC** for 2025 (actual observed trades, not NBBO). A provider-imposed free call rate limit occasionally interrupts collection. For one stock date pagination was required: PYPL Feb21 first 5m page stopped at 1:10pm ET, and the first stock stop occurred at **1:20pm ET on the next page**. Crucial: treating the first response page as a complete day would have incorrectly said there was NO stock stop. Stored time-aligned observations in `four_event_free_5min_timestamped_option_prints_2026-10-10.json`. When the stock's first qualifying **5-minute BAR HIGH/LOW** crossed a threshold, actual threshold touch may have occurred at any time within that bar. First option bar strictly AFTER the crossing bar is a delayed observed trade, NOT the bid available at the first touch.

### Especially important execution-risk example: QCOM rising
The September 5 stock target bar was 9:40 ET, but $170 option first traded afterward at 9:50 ET for $0.28. At 9:55 ET, the option five-minute trade range was **$0.36–$0.44**, at 10:00 ET **$0.43–$0.48**, then it subsequently sold off and its last trade was $0.16. FIRST after-signal print at $0.28 implies −37.3%, later $0.48 print would imply +8.5%, day-end $0.16 implies −64.8%. The instrument generated 3 very different hindsight "returns" on the SAME target day. None represents a known available bid or executable sale at the moment stock first touched target; the five-minute stock trigger is an interval, and option transaction prints may be sporadic.
