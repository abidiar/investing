# A genuinely non-lookahead historical call-entry audit: 55 earnings events, 2025
Completed and saved 2026-10-10. **Outcome: NO VERIFIED HISTORICAL OBSERVED 10:00–10:30 OPTION ENTRY under the frozen rule. This is NOT a 0% strategy success rate or a proof no quotes existed.**

## Error fixed
The original call pilot selected one OCC contract based on SAME-DAY FIRST reported option trade price and required >=10 option contracts traded over the ENTIRE stock-entry date, while claiming entry at the next 9:30 STOCK OPEN. Both the first actual option trade and the total future-day contract volume can occur/accumulate AFTER 09:30. The 52 subsequently EOD-option-ask preselected contracts ALSO used same-day 4pm option quotes to decide what to have bought that morning. These are lookahead errors even if historical price source is authentic. First-trade and last-trade daily prints also are not actionable bid/ask fills at stock entry/exit.

Pre-frozen alternate strategy in `frozen_nonlookahead_prior_close_1000am_historical_option_protocol_2026-10-10.md`. This is a NEW historically testable strategy (not a retrospective change of return for original 9:30 strategy). Keep historical prior-identified company events, day3 vs 3 nondecreasing lows plus prior 2 highs stock signals, and 30/60-calendar-day expiry windows, <=$50 purchase cap inclusive entry fee, 100-share CALL and OTM strike.

**Previous close at 4pm ET:** select strike using ONLY archival EOD stock close and option bid/ask from LAST COMPLETED stock-market session before frozen stock entry day, NO same-entry-day quote or volume. First observed expiry within entry+30..37 or entry+60..67 dates, nearest OTM strike up to 125% historical unadjusted underlying prior close. Demand actual prior-day option noncrossed bid/ask with prior ask *100 +$0.65 <=$50 and `(ask-bid)/ask<=35%`; no previous-day option trade volume or OI available in Dolt. This spread threshold 35% was PREDECLARED before new outcomes; never optimize on these historical results. The historical free option snapshots show only a few observed expirations per date, so absence means NOT OBSERVED IN FREE DATA, not unlisted at the exchange. Prior close ASK is a proxy for next-day affordability, NOT next-day live ask.
**10am gate:** option trade volume before entry time is sum of ACTUAL option 5-minute RTH option transactions 09:30–09:59:59 ET (not completed day) >=10 contracts. Select first actual option trade bar between 10:00 and 10:30 ET, inclusive; option *first trade*×100+$0.65 must remain <=$50. Trade bar time is a 5-minute interval and prints are NOT contemporaneous buy-side ask. Recheck OTM against underlying raw price at actual delayed entry minute if any entry passes. The +5%/-5% stock target stops START from this adjusted (delayed) stock ENTRY raw contemporaneous market price, not 9:30 open, then evaluate first threshold reach and next options trade/ideally actual NBBO. Missing timestamps never imputed. The original 2024/25 observations were already partly outcome-inspected and not an untouched holdout.

## Interpretation and next research priority
**We FIXED the as-of rule and tested it without violating time order.** The observed result is that the specific conservative prior-close + 10am activity strategy had no verifiable options transaction entry among 55 2025 selloff events in the data at hand. This does not prove the 10am contracts were unpurchasable: a posted bid/ask can permit trading without actual prints, and the free provider's 5m trade coverage can be empty. But we have **no observed purchased calls**, and cannot infer an expected option profit or count the missing as zero/wins/losses. It also does not invalidate the underlying-quality-companies-often-rebound hypothesis, only the currently testable low-cost CALL implementation with this narrow observable 10am liquidity gate.
The next real historical bottleneck is **actual historical 9:55–10:30 bid/ask snapshots or quotes and last completed trading day option volume/open interest**, combined with full historical as-of listed strikes. No working free complete source supplying all of those fields for 2025 issuers has been validated. If someone wants to relax 35% or the 10-contract by-10am gate or purchase later, freeze a NEW protocol first and evaluate on a DIFFERENT historical period/held-out companies, not tune on the same results.
No new shadow test, no paid subscription, no call scanner change.
