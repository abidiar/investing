## Additional mechanisms not measured in prior 164 study
- **Issuer new outside catalysts**: government/political public endorsement, procurement contract, certification/patent/regulatory surprise, material contract/partnership (AI infrastructure), competitor disruption. Need exact public headline timestamp and sector-matched intraday abnormal returns, not conclude price correlation proves single cause.
- **Issuer fundamental evolution**: orders/backlog, growing AI server units, gross and operating margin quality, analysts' as-of future-year EPS revisions, new guidance/production/supply contracts, renewed trust in ability to finance and execute and operating cashflow; a company can be profitable and yet radically overpriced before a miss.
- **Capital support**: executed and disclosed actual issuer share buybacks (not mere board authorization); activist or long-only institutions increasing stakes if PUBLIC before entry; short covering, ETF creation/redemption, float/borrow and derivatives hedging pressure.
- **Broad passive pensions/401k flows**: continuous dollar-cost averaging into index funds can supply structural capital to benchmark constituents, but does NOT mean pension funds specifically identify DELL as cheap after Q2 miss. Passive buy orders allocated across constituents by index weights, sector exposure and net flows; can be overwhelmed by active selling. Verify eligibility/index membership as-of historical date before assuming, and no event-level direct flow to specific stock measured.
- **Systematic market factor recovery**: SPY/SMH/XLK rising or easing rates, stronger cloud capex, Nvidia/Oracle earnings and outlook readthrough to Dell orders. Sector first three days already weak predictive in original matched164; FUTURE sector rise cannot be day4 signal.
