1. Both green `close > open`; both higher closes `close_b > close_a` and second low **not lower** than first low: `low_b >= low_a`. Do NOT require a same-low retracement, a prior-day SMA, a VWAP reclaim or a 3rd candle.
2. Second bullish bar CLOSE > high_a (positive buyer-initiation break), and its total volume >= MEDIAN volume of the THREE previous five-minute bars `k−3...k−1` (observable at close_b). This is a modest volume-confirmation proxy, NOT actual signed aggressive buyer order-flow or proof of selling exhaustion.
