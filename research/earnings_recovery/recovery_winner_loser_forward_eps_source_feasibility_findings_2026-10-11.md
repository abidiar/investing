# Earnings recovery case-control: first findings and point-in-time forward EPS revision feasibility
2026-10-11 | Status: **EXPLORATORY MECHANISM FINDINGS; REAL EPS REVISIONS NOT YET MEASURED; NO OPTIONS EDGE**

## Corrected research question
Not 'what do eventual recovered stocks share?' alone. The correct test is: Among EVERY objectively initially-qualified high-quality after-hours earnings selloff, which pre-D4 *information-valid* characteristics discriminate (A) buy D4 +3% stock high before -5% low within 20 sessions vs failure and separately (B) medium/long-run price partial/full recovery? Do not infer buying an option is profitable just because full recovery later touched. Winners plus same-universe/sector/year/announcement-time losers; no outcome-selected only-rebound cohorts.

## Real historical D3-observable case-control completed
Previously quality-qualified afterhours events N91 in 2023–June2026. Already outcome-exposed; 54 had LOWER intraday LOW during D1–D3 below initial qualifying >=5% selloff day D0; D4 open underlying +3% profit / -5% stop, 20 sessions: **38 targets-first; 16 stop-first**. Compared these exact cases on D3-known features (only dated price history + 2023–26 SPY quotes, same stock and ETF dates). Frozen exploratory variable families before the NEW median comparisons at `frozen_d3_asof_winner_failure_feature_audit_2026-10-11.md`.
Medians winner (n38) vs stop (n16):
- Original AH earnings selloff magnitude from earnings-day close to objectively qualifying D0 close: **-8.024% vs -8.510%**. (Important: D0 sometimes second post-earnings session; correct D0/earnings index mapping, not D0/prior day close.)
- Worse additional LOW within D1–D3 relative D0 LOW: **-2.265% vs -3.391%**.
- D3 close above as-of trough D0..D3: **+1.697% vs +1.633%**.
- D0-D3 underlying RTH return minus SPY close/close: **-2.196pp vs -2.071pp**.
- D0-D3 stock closing change: -1.985% vs -1.700%; daily mean 3-day range: 3.225% vs 3.092%.
These price factors DO NOT obviously separate winners from losses, are NOT independently stable discriminants, and were already used for other research filters. Do NOT invent a cutoff for extra drawdown or relative strength based on these medians. Within original/holdout company strata directions differ, with only 3 disjoint group failures. No classifier, statistical efficacy, call options, or non-overlapping validation run.
The retrospective future 60-day +5%-from-D0-CLOSE high among all AH was **65/91**. Among 54 early-dip group, 35/54 eventually hit that level; **6/16 D4 stop-outs eventually had that +5% D0-reference rebound**, and **9/38 D4 winners did not reach that D0-reference +5% threshold within D60**. The distinction shows target condition must be actual D4-trade outcome instead of retrospective unconditional chart recovery.

## Historical analyst FY1 EPS revisions — exact requirement and provider audit
Need *same future fiscal period* (e.g., FY2025 EPS, or next not-yet-reported fiscal quarter) two timestamped cross-sectional consensus snapshots:
S0: analyst consensus published at or before AH release; S3: most recent consensus actually observable by D3 END, never later. Record ticker, eventDate, fiscalPeriodIdentifier, currency/accounting EPS type (GAAP vs adjusted), vendor definition, analystCount, timestampAsOf, sourceRetrievalTimestamp, consensusEPS, individual up/down changes or numerator, coverage. Require no roll of current-quarter-after-report onto a different projected quarter. Percent revision =100*(S3-S0)/abs(S0) only if positive safe EPS denominator; otherwise use sign/absolute dollar difference to avoid near-zero or negative denominator instability. Also inspect changed analyst count and stale samples. All absent data = MISSING, never 'unchanged'.
Actual tool/source checks on 2026-10-11:
- Webull connected `get_stock_forecast_eps` live META and DELL yielded their latest 4 reported quarters plus one upcoming projected quarter, **NO timestamped historic daily EPS consensus snapshots** for FY1, no date of analyst forecast change. Does not retroactively fill 2023–26 study.
- TipRanks get_recent_analyst_ratings reports monthly cap 10/10 consumed until 2026-11-01. Even if available, dated *price target* and buy/sell rating actions are not revisions to next-quarter/FY1 EPS and shouldn't be mislabeled.
- Alpha Vantage lists `EARNINGS_ESTIMATES` endpoint as including revision counts, but the public demo endpoint returned a demo-only sign-up instruction, and trailing 7d/30d revisions displayed NOW cannot reconstruct a 2024 D3 state without archival snapshots; historical as-of access remains UNVERIFIED. https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/
- FMP analyst estimate API docs expose fiscal report *period-end dates*, which cannot be treated as historical revision observation timestamps. Need verified as-of query / revision log before using any stored historical 'estimate'. https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/stable/analyst-estimates and https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/de/faqs?code=analyst.
- Proper archival historical daily consensus with observation date plus individual estimate change timestamps exists commercially via Zacks historical data / WRDS https://www.zackspro.com/historical.asp and https://zacksdata.com/datasets/consensus-data/. No connected free entitlement demonstrated and user does not wish to pay for data. DO NOT imply the dataset is impossible to obtain generally or that paid records are automatically accessible.
- Prior study `forward_guidance_proxy_results_2026-10-10.md`: comparable original *management* forward guidance existed for only 27/72 historical cases, 'stable/up' actually resulted in 5/23 D3 +5/-5 targets, gross -2.30%; failed as a stand-in. Do NOT re-test identical proxy hoping for different conclusion.

## Proposed research architecture (NOT a tested rule)
1. Lock a broad at-earnings-date issuer universe, not just remembered DELL/META/ORCL/QCOM cases. Preserve EVERY initially >=5% AH R1/R2 loss. Reconstruct historical quality using ONLY reports published before earnings, explicitly include former/delisted firms when available. Keep quality and nonquality event data separate.
2. On each D3, freeze candidate factor row: original earnings severity, preannouncement financial quality and past revisions/earnings surprise, timestamped future EPS consensus change only when available, broad market/sector-adjusted return and volatility, sector news, stock return relative to prior-day as-of low; missing as missing.
3. Outcomes thereafter: PRIMARY same +3% before -5% D4 opening STOCK trade 20 sessions net modeled costs and never ignoring stopped events. SECONDARY +5% from D0 close within D60 and full recovery preearnings close within D60/120 as separate labels. Options premium affordability/bid-ask/IV/expiry is a wholly separate *third* outcome.
4. Discovery: compare all winners and losers, medians and missingness. Fit at most a tiny preregistered predictive screen/model with company-group separation; under current limited numbers do NOT train 20-factor ML. Evaluate improvement over simple D4 across all AH events, baseline risk/return, per-signal expectancy with skipped events zero, liquidity/execution and independent unseen years/issuers.
5. Validation: truly later/untouched events with original earnings dates and predeclared snapshot schedule; don't cherry-pick rebound companies or redesign based on their outcomes. Capture data prospectively if genuinely unavailable historically, with timestamped daily forecast revisions and vendor dates, not posthoc refreshed historical API fields.
6. No option call alert, no cheap-call DTE selection, no automation changes based on this pilot. Do not pay for data.
