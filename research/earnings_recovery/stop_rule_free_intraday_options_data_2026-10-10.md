# Earnings rebound options: no-cost historical quote-data stop decision

Decision date: 2026-10-10. This research phase is CLOSED, not validated. Do not restart an exhaustive search for free historical 2025 issuer-specific intraday option quotes without a concrete new source.

Evidence: Four-company contracts, 100-event 2024/25 free EOD quotes, 52 frozen historical volume checks, a dividend/unadjusted-stock-barrier audit, and a 55-event 2025 previous-close / 10am non-lookahead experiment are archived and reproducible in the earnings_recovery research folder. Of 52 prior EOD ask-selected call cells, 37 option trade histories were returned, 17 passed retrospective first reported trade cost <= $50 and full entry-day 10-contract volume, and 15 had date-last-trade marks. No observed historical execution or unbiased return was established. The frozen previous-close 55-event 2025 experiment had 101 stock signals, only nine prior-close-affordable/spread-qualified calls, and ZERO calls with verified historical option prints passing the entire 10am transaction gate: four low early volume, one no 10-10:30 option trade, and four missing intraday trade data. This is NOT a 0% win rate.

Provider verification: Massive historical option quotes /v3/quotes/optionsTicker are excluded from the no-cost Options Basic plan (and prior call NOT_ENTITLED). ThetaData Free supplies EOD but historical minute NBBO is paid. Alpaca Free indicative option quotes are NOT actual OPRA historical NBBO. No validated free timestamped quote database was found. Sources: https://www.massive.com/docs/rest/options/trades-quotes/quotes ; https://docs.thetadata.us/Articles/Getting-Started/Subscriptions.html ; https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/historical-option-data .

Decision: STOP historical NBBO acquisition searches; do not promote calls to scanner or claim $50 call strategy profitability. No paid dataset, forward shadow test, or scanner promotion. The next meaningful simple priority, only if further research is wanted: test ONE existing stock-level post-earnings recovery signal against a simple baseline for unadjusted +5% BEFORE -5% on an actually unseen post-June-2026 earnings cohort, freezing a cohort before seeing forward price outcomes. Show actual incidence, time to target, stop-outs, and omissions; do not equate stock gains to call gains. Avoid new elaborate strategy variants without evidence of stock edge.
