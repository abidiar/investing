# Frozen prior-close and 10:00 ET option entry timing protocol
Date frozen before retrieving new prior-close chains and time-of-day option trade returns: 2026-10-10. No prospective/shadow test, paid data or post-hoc ticker selection.

## Why new protocol
Previous stock entries were at next STOCK open, whereas call price used the first OPTION trade of the same day and >=10 option contracts by END OF DAY. Both are unavailable at the stock-opening decision timestamp and introduce lookahead. Prior 52 EOD-ask-selected candidate calls also leak same-entry-day closing quotes into strike selection. Treat the previous 52 test as retrospective liquidity exploration only; do NOT relabel it as a tradeable strategy.

## Universe, as-of sources and timing
- All previously quality-qualified 2025 selloff events in the canonical 164-event OHLC archive, 55 events (29 original92; 26 holdout72) × frozen day3 & first three nondecreasing lows plus prior-two-high breakout stock-signal dates, including no signals and missing quotes. Keep first underlying signal next-session date and expiration windows +30–37/+60–67 calendar days; do not use exit outcomes to select contracts. The 2024 events can be carried later under same rules, but 2024 intraday option trade history is often NOT_ENTITLED; report coverage separately, don't mix it.
- **Pre-selection at the 4:00pm ET close of the trading day BEFORE the stock entry date**: choose only from the DoltHub date-specific END-OF-DAY option-chain snapshot of that previous trading session, and historical as-of-day unadjusted underlying closing price. Sources must be date-addressed as-of the old session, not current live contract snapshots.
- Valid standard 100-share CALL, unadjusted strike strictly above prior underlying regular daily CLOSE, at most 125% of that CLOSE; expiration must be the earliest snapshot-observed expiry in fixed 30 or 60 calendar-days [entry+30, entry+37] / [entry+60, entry+67] window. An expiry is chosen BEFORE quote or affordability filter; if no observed expiry mark NOT_OBSERVED, not 'unlisted'. For each expiry choose CLOSEST-TO-PRIOR-CLOSE strike (smallest OTM) among options with yesterday's valid positive, noncrossed bid/ask, yesterday ask×100 + $0.65 <= $50, yesterday (ask-bid)/ask <= **35%**. This is a pre-declared *prior-close liquidity proxy*, not verified previous-day traded-volume or open interest. If no such quote, NO_PREENTRY_LIQUID_CONTRACT_OBSERVED. Do not use any entry-day EOD quote to select.
- 10:00am ET (America/New_York) is the option entry gate: no option purchase before 10:00. Require historical regular-session 5-minute option-trade bars starting 09:30 to 09:55 whose sum of traded contract counts `v` >=10 **before** 10:00. Include no bars outside 09:30–09:59. Choose the FIRST actually printed eligible option-trade bar starting 10:00 through 10:30 ET, inclusive, as a *timestamped price proxy*, not confirmed buy ASK; require its first reported trade/open ×100+$0.65 <= $50. If missing bars, low prior trade volume, or premium > cap => NO_ENTRY_VERIFIED or FAIL; never carry forward later bars or infer prices. No intraday bid/ask = mark ASK_EXECUTION_UNVERIFIED separately. The option bar at 10:00 represents possible within-bar execution after 10:00, not exact 10:00 fill.
- Underlying entry reference is the **stock's first eligible same or later 5-minute bar opening** at the option trade bar timestamp, using unadjusted RTH stock bars. Contract must still be strictly OTM vs this reference stock price and strike<=1.25×stock price; if missing stock bar/stock price, unresolved and not eligible. 2025 next-open signal may have been at 09:30 but OPTION positions/underlying target/stop start ONLY after actual delayed 10:00+ option trade proxy. This is a new alternate strategy, not an overlay on 9:30 returns.
- Stock +5% profit target and -5% stop of NEW time-aligned unadjusted underlying reference, first touch within 20 *trading sessions* starting entry, stop-first if high and low same intraday bar. At first stock target/stop FIVE-MINUTE bar, seek actual option trade in **first 5-minute bar STRICTLY AFTER stock crossing bar**, <=30 minutes later, else outcome MISSING_TIME_ALIGNED_OPTION_EXIT and no forced exit price. If no stock threshold by end 20 sessions, exit last observed option trade after scheduled stock close (or expire/deliverable adjustment cutoff), with timing caveat. Do not substitute EOD last trade on earlier adjusted-stock exit dates. Validate split/cash dividends with raw OHLC and OCC deliverables.
- Option position historical purchase cost/revenues use illustrative $0.65 on each side, but OPTION PRINTS ARE NOT EXECUTABLE ASK/BID; separately label all such returns observational and do not promote signals.
- Evaluate ALL 55*2*2=220 nominal method×expiry cells with stage attrition: NO_SIGNAL, MISSING_PREV_STOCK_CLOSE, MISSING_PREV_CHAIN, MISSING_EXPIRY, NO_LIQUID_PREV_CANDIDATE, 10AM_NOT_ENOUGH_VOLUME, NO_10AM_TRADE, >$50 10AM_PRINT, STOCK_OTM_FAIL, MISSING_TIME_ALIGNED_EXIT, print proxy positive/negative; count unique underlying events and unique call selections, no double count across 30/60 DTE. Missing data never zero, and conditional ROI cannot be interpreted as full-universe expectancy.
