## ONE nonlookahead first failed-new-low setup
At FIRST qualifying -5% five-minute bar `i`, record that candle's low L0 and traded volume V0 (both available at its 5m close). It is the FIRST breach bar, not a hindsight session trough. No subsequent lowering of the anchor to later lows.
- Seek the EARLIEST subsequent five-minute candle j between i+2 and i+12 inclusive, with RTH bar start <=12:30 ET, having `L_j >= 0.998*L0` and `L_j <= 1.003*L0` (retest within 0.2% below / 0.3% above first low) and `V_j <= 0.80*V0` (selling-volume contraction proxy). This first matching `j` is the frozen retest; do not use an earlier unconfirmed lower low as a future price anchor, and do not select a later `j` based on future outcome if this fails.
- From completed bars j+1 through j+3, seek FIRST green candle k with `close_k > open_k` and `close_k > max(high_j,high_{j-1})` and `volume_k >=1.20 * mean(volume_{k-3..k-1})`. This is observable at k CLOSE. No conditional future candle information.
- Enter at the actual OPEN of the NEXT five-minute stock candle k+1, same session, only if the entry opening price is <=1.015×L0 and after 09:30, before/equal 13:05 ET. If overextended, skip; do not rescan or pretend fill before breakout candle closed. Limit to one contract-agnostic underlying entry per event.
- A signal can FAIL despite favorable later price action; count skipped as no-trade.
