## Comparison — one hypothesis only
**Benchmark:** third *following* trading-session open after the qualifying selloff-day close (day3), not the selloff closing low.
**Confirmation signal:** earliest post-selloff session index 3 through 20 that shows three nondecreasing lows and close ABOVE maximum high of prior two sessions, buy NEXT trading-session open. Skip if no trigger by selloff+20 or if first triggered next open >8% above frozen selloff-day close; no second trigger search.
**Stock exit:** unadjusted as-traded historical open/high/low/close, historical split-normalized basis only when appropriate, +5% profit barrier and −5% stop from respective trade entry OPEN, over 20 entry-inclusive trading sessions, OPEN gap executed first, STOP wins a daily OHLC bar that intersects both barriers, end-of-window time exit at bar 20 close. IMPORTANT: use as-traded price events (cash-dividend UNadjusted), NOT Adj Close series, and flag splits/deliverables during hold.
**Outcome:** for each event and method, count +5%-before−5%, −5% stop first, or time exit; report entry coverage, day-to-target, P&L of individual stock trade percent excluding fees/slippage, plus mean return **per ALL frozen eligible selloff events** (no-trigger => zero) as well as per actually triggered signal. The paired benchmark includes the exact same qualifying events. No options ROI calculations.
**Decision rule predeclared:** Require at least 20 distinct eligible events, >=60% target-before-stop of ALL events for confirmation signal, at least +10 percentage points advantage over baseline target rate, plus positive paired mean per-event gross return, before recommending ANY further confirmation signal work. Even if gates pass, a single late-2026 period is too small for production deployment; no A+ promotion until independent replication and realistic execution costs. When n<20 label underpowered, never extrapolate an A+ probability.
