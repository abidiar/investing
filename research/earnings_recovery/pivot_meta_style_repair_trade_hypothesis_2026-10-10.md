# Pivot: test the actual META-style recovery trade, not a blanket earnings-dip call

Research direction checkpoint — 2026-10-10. **Hypothesis only; not yet demonstrated profitable.**

## What old results do / do not say
The 15-event July-Sep 2026 frozen test compared third post-earnings-session STOCK OPEN against delayed three-rising-lows breakout and required +5% STOCK before -5% STOCK over 20 sessions. It did not include META, DELL or ORCL. It says nothing directly about 1–3-day cheap-call momentum-repricing trades. The 164-event historical panel found 113/164 quality earnings selloffs touched +5% within 60 sessions, often after large interim drawdowns. The four exemplars DELL/META/QCOM/ORCL were hindsight-mentioned, with 16/19 touching +5% within 60 sessions, NOT a viable option fill study.

User-reported / previously discussed ACTUAL options examples should be separated by trade mechanism: META July 10 2026 near-expiration $655 call sold at approximately $21 premium with inferred cost around $1.02 (real screenshot discussed, purchase inferred, NOT a guaranteed reproducible return); AAPL September 18 $342.50 call entered ~$0.41 and sold ~$0.78 (+90% gross); UBER September 18 $74 call entered ~$0.55 and sold ~$1.02 (+85% gross); PYPL scalp ~$0.56→$0.64 (+14%). Do NOT call these all post-earnings recoveries. Separate overnight momentum/gap, catalyst-washout, and earnings swing classifications by event. Include losing trades, cases not taken and same-name failed signals; otherwise survivor-selected profit case studies tell us no hit rate.

## One next testable candidate setup: quality dislocation -> price and relative-strength repair
Watchlist eligibility before signal:
- Prior known fundamentals reasonably intact; >=5% earnings/catalyst selloff or multi-day displacement (state catalyst and time) and no clearly thesis-invalidating guide/revisions. Fundamental label must be independently dated and available at entry.
- Price has stopped making immediate new lows and shows a higher intraday low; 5-minute acceptance above VWAP / meaningful morning resistance; relative strength to SPY AND own sector ETF improves in the same session; adequate remaining price runway to resistance, no huge extension from repair.
- No trade unless an actual <=$50 all-in standard listed call with 2–4 weeks left, approx >=0.20 delta and acceptable spread exists at the signal. A short-dated 0DTE trade like historic META is a SEPARATE much-higher-risk mechanism, not automatically included in this small-account template.

One primary STOCK-measurable proxy research objective: conditional on the FIRST qualifying repair signal, what fraction achieves **+1.5% in underlying before −1.0%** within THREE entry-inclusive sessions? Not option ROI; the option could win or lose depending on Greeks, spread, timing. Freeze one threshold set on development, then test matched same-stock selloff events without repair / other market days on separate companies or future unseen period. Exact 5-minute signal must be fully observable at entry, with time-stamped SPY/sector bars and no same-day hindsight volume. Count event coverage including zero-trade cases, stop-first same-bar, and don't tune multiple thresholds on outcomes. As soon as reliable timestamped actual options bid/ask becomes accessible WITHOUT subscription, a further test may target +20-30% net call sale before -20-25% option drawdown using actual fills/quotes, not stock surrogate.

**Important:** The 1.5%/-1.0%/3-day hypothesis is an operational proposed new test, not a known or proven optimal parameter combination. A small count of successful personal trades does NOT establish positive expectancy or representative win probability. However the prior fixed day-three +5%/-5% test did not falsify this more precise mechanism. Do not promote scanner or buy data.
