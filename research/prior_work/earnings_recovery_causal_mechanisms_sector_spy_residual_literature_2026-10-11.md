# Literature before pre-D4 recovery drivers sector/risk/liquidity test
Date 2026-10-11. QUESTION: Which features EXIST AS OF D3 END distinguish ultimate FULL 60d recovery from nonrecovery after a >5% quality-company earnings reaction, and which identify a +3%-before−5% D4 stock trade? Distinguish valuation price changes due to EXPECTED CASHFLOW REVISION, changing required discount rates/macro/industry premiums, temporary nonfundamental liquidity flow and behavioral sentiment, company earnings surprise relative to forecast and changes in management tone. Never infer causal investor motivation from OHLC alone.

Best CLOSE prior research:
1. Da, Liu, Schaumburg (2014) "A Closer Look at the Short-Term Return Reversal", *Management Science* 60(3):658–674, https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2013.1766 ; original Fed NY research report https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr513.html . Separates industry return momentum, industry-level variation in expected returns, delayed CASHFLOW news and residual. Those recent price changes not supported by fundamentals more often reverse; their residual long-side positive result attributed to liquidity shocks, short-side to sentiment. This is NOT a quality AH earnings D4 call test but remarkably close mechanism. Their analyst-based fundamental decomp uses I/B/E/S revision history not present in free connected APIs; *sector-adjusted* recent return is NOT the complete nonfundamental residual.
2. Price et al (2012) "Earnings conference calls and stock returns: The incremental informativeness of textual tone" *Journal of Banking and Finance* 36:992-1011 https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378426611002901 : Q&A call language predicts subsequent returns beyond EPS surprise over ~60d. Most of this historical call evidence is not available free via Quartr, connection requires Pro.
3. Noh & Zhou (2022) "Executives' Blaming external factors and market reactions: Evidence from earnings conference calls", Journal of Banking & Finance 134, 106358 https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378426621003095 . Executives blaming bad performance on economic or INDUSTRY conditions predicts negative non-reversing abnormal returns and subsequent analyst downgrades, showing 'it's just macro' can be an adverse forward profit signal. No transcript here.
4. Huang (2022) doctoral dissertation on forecast revisions https://escholarship.org/uc/item/2r7980f3 supports aggregated contemporaneous forward analyst revision over historical earnings surprise in predicting drift. No historical as-of S0/S3 quarterly FY estimates via existing free Webull.
5. JFE "Cashflow risk, systematic earnings revisions, and the cross-section of stock returns" (2009), https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X0900141X : company returns partly driven by common expected cash flow revisions, why sector/market tailwind can't be assumed independent from fundamentals.
6. Pan, Sul, Wang (2026), *Review of Accounting Studies* https://doi.org/10.1007/s11142-026-09959-y : delayed discovery of market macro information within three trading days for large earnings surprise, gone ~day7. Motivates D0–D3 MARKET and sector returns as the as-of input window.
