# Literature adjudication: Does existing work answer the EXACT D4–D5 post-earnings oversold-call question?
Research date 2026-10-11. **Verdict: NO direct peer-reviewed validation found for our full strategy; several high-relevance published mechanisms answer crucial pieces, sometimes with opposite implications.** This is an existing-research review, not a trade backtest. Search focused on short-term reversal vs continuation CONDITIONAL ON newly disclosed earnings information.

## Exact decision under review
A pre-announcement financially sound U.S. stock reports earnings AFTER market CLOSE and experiences >=5% initial RTH closing selloff, often additional selling D1..D3. From facts available BY D3 CLOSE (before buying D4/D5), distinguish +3% STOCK target hitting before -5% stock stop within 20 sessions from further collapse and determine if $50-or-less call would profit after real bid/ask/IV/theta. Needs equal-period pre-earnings earnings/financial guidance vs newly released surprises, market/sector controls, prior-expectations revisions, first-hit OHLC/call quote outcomes, ex ante test.

## Directly relevant peer-reviewed studies (not direct D4 validation)
1. **Das, S., Kim, K., Patro, S. (2012)**, "On the Anomalous Stock Price Response to Management Earnings Forecasts", *Journal of Business Finance & Accounting* 39:905-935, DOI https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-5957.2012.02298.x . The abstract finds economically meaningful upward post-forecast price drift following both GOOD and BAD management quarterly earnings forecasts, with negative relation between initial market reaction and subsequent post-guidance drift for BAD forecasts, consistent with REVERSAL of initial overreaction, and estimated-cost-aware strategy profits. **Closest published mechanism for selective overshoot.** But forecast-release events ≠ AH >=5% quality screened stock selloffs with D4 next open and cheap calls; no such success rate documented.
2. **Druz, M., Wagner, A. F., Zeckhauser, R. J. (2020)**, "When Managers Change Their Tone, Analysts and Investors Change Their Tune", *Financial Analysts Journal* 76(2), DOI https://doi.org/10.1080/0015198X.2019.1707592 ; practitioner methods/summary https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/research/financial-analysts-journal/2020/ip-when-managers-change-their-tone . Unusually BLEAK CONFERENCE-CALL managerial TONE CHANGES forecast LOWER future earnings and HIGHER uncertainty, and negative drift/underperformance after initial reaction; conditional on other factors and controlling for press release tone and analysts' questions. CFA summary quantifies one-SD negative tone shift, roughly next-quarter analyst consensus -1.85%, announcement-window abnormal stock -0.55%, subsequently benchmark underperformance; profitability discussed in monthly dollar-neutral portfolios. **Closest to spotting continuing fundamental downside and the NEED to compare tone with previous baseline**, not simply count negative words or judge static 'demand concerns'.
3. **Huang, Kanyuan (2022)** dissertation "The Value of Analyst Forecast Revisions: Evidence from Earnings Announcements", UC Berkeley https://escholarship.org/uc/item/2r7980f3 . Aggregate analyst EPS forecast changes sorted post-event showed stronger later drift than earnings-surprise sorting, especially large magnitude events and analyst revisions that CONTRADICT initial earnings surprise. **Most plausible information mismatch signal**, but dissertation evidence and longer drift, not a D4 option strategy; access to timestamped FY1 S0 vs D3 S3 consensus is presently unverified/free-data-blocked.
4. **Zhang (2008)**, "Analyst Responsiveness and the Post-Earnings-Announcement Drift", *Journal of Accounting and Economics* 46(1):201–215 https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacceco.2008.04.004 . Analysts revising next-quarter forecasts within 2 trading days speed incorporation of news and attenuate subsequent drift. **Timestamp matters; DON'T backfill post-D3 ratings/forecasts as D3 input.**
5. **Price, S. McKay, Doran, J. S., Peterson, D. R., Bliss, B. A. (2012)** "Earnings Conference Calls and Stock Returns: The Incremental Informativeness of Textual Tone", *Journal of Banking & Finance* 36(4):992-1011 https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378426611002901 . Original call Q&A tone adds information for post-announcement abnormal returns beyond EPS surprise and prepared statements over 60 trading days. NOT specific 4th-day reversal, but means TEXT from the actual same-day or next-day call should not be omitted from the earnings-news audit if it existed by D3.
6. **Feldman, Govindaraj, Livnat, Segal (2010)** "Management's tone change, post earnings announcement drift and accruals", *Review of Accounting Studies* 15, https://www.insead.edu/faculty-research/publications/journal-articles/managements-tone-change-post-earnings-announcement . MD&A tone change versus prior 10-Q or 10-K predicts post-FILING drift beyond surprises and accruals, BUT SEC 10-Q can be FILED AFTER D3! Strictly NO treating such later MD&A as known for D4 trades.
7. **Bathke, Mason, Morton (2019)** "Investor Overreaction to Earnings Surprises and Post-Earnings-Announcement Reversals", *Contemporary Accounting Research* 36(4):2069-2092 https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1911-3846.12491 . Particular weak seasonal EPS persistence cases show earnings-surprise reversals **at the NEXT quarter's announcement**, not necessarily D4; attractive conceptual boundary and potential earnings persistence measure, but timing mismatch.
8. **Kottimukkalur (2019)** "Attention to Market Information and Underreaction to Earnings on Market Moving Days", *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis* https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/abs/attention-to-market-information-and-underreaction-to-earnings-on-market-moving-days/10D872F0E8756FF1C84971CA0C9B9B2F . Negative surprise drift is MORE negative in strong broad market moving days, and lower analyst coverage/high prior value. Regime/attention confound, not automatic rebound.
9. **Chordia, Goyal, Sadka, Shivakumar (2009)**, "Liquidity and the Post-Earnings-Announcement Drift", *Financial Analysts Journal* https://business.columbia.edu/faculty/research/liquidity-and-post-earnings-announcement-drift . Trading costs absorb 70–100% of classic long-short paper PEAD profit among illiquid shares. Small-account far-OTM calls have even more contract-specific spread/IV/theta challenges; not modeled by above stock studies.
10. **Fink (2021)** PEAD review https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbef.2020.100446 . Known underreaction to actual negative earnings surprises can cause continued weakness; expecting universal rebound conflicts with this literature.

## Precise answer and next research step
**NO** located study jointly reports (i) quality-qualified >5% AH earnings selloff, (ii) condition of new low D1–D3, (iii) *original-release and call* surprise/tone/revisions observable by D3, (iv) entry D4 or D5 with 3% target before -5% stop, (v) option's historical actual strike/expiry ASK-to-BID IV/cost/cheap premium. The papers teach mechanisms for reversal AND continuation, not one reliable completed executable options rule. They suggest ONE important, predeclared candidate discrimination:
- POSITIVE discordance = stock suffered large negative *market-relative* reaction but newly announced **future** earnings guidance/analyst EXPECTATION CHANGE or call tone vs its PRIOR baseline was *less negative than market pricing implied*;
- NEGATIVE confirmation = forward analyst estimates still sliding and call managerial tone unexpectedly BLEAK vs firm's own prior calls, signifying continuing impairment.
BUT **'market pricing implied' is a new model** requiring peer/sector, value multiple, and expectation metric; no established scalar from this literature. Need pre-register observable variables, source dates, baseline controls, matching, independent calendar/issuer events, D4 first-hit stock outcomes and options P&L, minimum n, and carefully handle missing historical EPS snapshots.
This review is LITERATURE ONLY; no new scanner thresholds or trading advice. Do not falsely present management binary outlook as validated: previously FAILED stand-alone when 27/72 known.
