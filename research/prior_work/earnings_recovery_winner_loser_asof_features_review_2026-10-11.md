# Prior-work and feasibility review: recovered-vs-failed earnings selloff case-control
Date 2026-10-11. The research is NOT "pick stocks already known to have recovered." It is "at the moment D3 closes, which information differentiates subsequently TRADEABLE early rebound winners and nonwinners?"

## Empirical grounding and limitations
- Fink (2021) review https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbef.2020.100446: bad surprises can drift lower rather than reverse.
- Kanyuan Huang (2022), dissertation https://escholarship.org/uc/item/2r7980f3: aggregated analyst earnings forecast revisions after announcements associated with future abnormal returns; high relevance to future earnings expectations, but not proof of D4 call ROI.
- JAE 2008 *Analyst Responsiveness and the Post-Earnings-Announcement Drift* https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacceco.2008.04.004: revisions' timing matters.
- Chordia et al. (2009) *Liquidity and the Post-Earnings-Announcement Drift*: spread and trading costs matter (https://business.columbia.edu/faculty/research/liquidity-and-post-earnings-announcement-drift).
- Our own `forward_guidance_proxy_results_2026-10-10.md`: 27/72 original management guidance comparable, stable/up was NOT a validated D3 stock edge; no analyst consensus snapshots.
- Our own `recovery_onset_164event_rs_stabilization_factor_results_2026-10-10.md`: simple later SMA5/three rising lows and 3d price relative strength filters failed or underperformed against earlier baselines; the new audit only asks descriptive AS-OF-D3 discriminative relationship, not a promotion of old rejected rule.
- Our own `asof_near_low_limit_ah_v1_findings_2026-10-11.md`: 54 AH cases with fresh low by D3, D4 OPEN +3%/-5%/20s resulted 38 winners/16 stop losses in outcome-exposed cohort; company independent but time overlap. 2022 new-issuer test had 4/7 target, -0.63% net and was underpowered. Avoid selecting only rebound survivors.
- Historical current fiscal-year EPS estimate from a current API is NOT comparable to an analyst EPS estimate first published before release; same fiscal target and as-of timestamp required.
- Zacks institutional dataset historically archives DAILY consensus EPS with observation date, FY/quarter, broker revision timestamp etc, but proprietary product: https://www.zackspro.com/historical.asp and https://zacksdata.com/datasets/consensus-data/ ; no current free connector access.
- Alpha Vantage docs have EARNINGS_ESTIMATES trailing rolling 7-/30-day revision counts, but demo key API returned "claim key"; cannot use current trailing7 to reconstruct prior 2024 D0 and D3 snapshots https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/.
- FMP estimates endpoint exposes fiscal-end date and aggregates; cannot simply interpret forecast period end date as an historical revision observation date https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/de/faqs?code=analyst.
- Live connector feasibility tested: Webull current forecast_eps for META/DELL returns latest 4 actual reported + ONE upcoming projected quarter, no as-of snapshots, dates of individual analyst revisions, or multiple fiscal year forward consensus for old 2023–26 cases. TipRanks recent ratings tool says monthly cap 10/10 exhausted until November 1 2026 UTC; rating target actions would be price targets, not fiscal EPS revisions. Previous tests of data providers (historical archive financial-data credits/Quartr paid) failed free historical coverage. Do not spend money, reconstruct fake revisions, or ask to retry rate-limited TipRanks.
- Future historical snapshot audit is valuable only if the provider demonstrably returns TWO OBSERVATIONS for the SAME future fiscal period with reliable publication/observation timestamps, one before earnings and one no later than D3 end. If insufficient, mark UNKNOWN and run a future prospective daily snapshot collector with exact collection timestamp (though provider refresh may lag).

## Causal/selection issue
Retrospectively profiling only stocks that recovered creates selection-on-the-outcome and can find universal characteristics of large companies, technology stocks, survivorship or industry/macro rebounds. Preserve event-level matched denominator including all failures and no-rebound cases, with issuer-year groupings and as-of cutoff. Full 60-session pre-earnings close recovery is different label from D4 +3-before-5, which is different from call option ask-to-bid profit.

## Scope of exploratory follow-up
On all 91 AH initial selloff cases, classify known-as-of-D3 early dip, then within 54 eligible classify actually executable stock D4 target-first versus stop-first; compare point-in-time stock OHLC price: original closing shock magnitude, D1-D3 worst low, D3 close distance above known lowest low, D3 advance relative to D0, D3 vs SPY same observed window, D0-D3 average daily range. Compute medians for wins/failures and cohort splits; do not create optimized thresholds or claim predictors on these already observed cases. Reserve truly independent multi-year validation for any prospective rule.
