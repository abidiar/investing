# Prior literature & already-tested techniques before D3 stock indicators
Date 2026-10-11. Question: Can simple AS-OF-D3 price-derived indicators distinguish recoverable postearnings selloff from continued breakdown (full 60-day stock PRE-earnings close reclaim), and an actually profitable D4-open +3% target before −5% stop over 20 sessions? Indicators measure price behavior/participation, NOT changes in consensus future EPS or persistent cash flows.

## Published independent research
- Fang, Qin, Jacobsen (2014) "Technical market indicators: An overview", *Journal of Behavioral and Experimental Finance* https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214635014000495 . Evaluated 93 broad market technical/sentiment indicators, found little robust return predictability even under favorable modeling assumptions, warns data snooping from many indicators / backtest variants. Broad market not specifically quality earnings gap/reversal and not individual-stock D3 RSI.
- Da, Liu and Schaumburg (2014) "A Closer Look at the Short-Term Return Reversal", *Management Science*, https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2013.1766 ; returns unexplained by CASHFLOW news/industry components tend to reverse, especially liquidity-induced *long* signals, highlighting that moving-average / RSI is a PROXY not a fundamental driver.
- Charles Schwab education on VWAP https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/how-to-use-volume-weighted-indicators-trading . Intraday VWAP = volume weighted price since OPEN, many execution algos benchmark against it; trading ABOVE VWAP can reflect net price acceptance but does not identify institutional intent, causally prove buy imbalance or predict next day profits. Requires OHLCV/TICK volume intraday, NOT present in existing earnings 164 daily OHLC arrays. Daily VWAP meaningless to reconstruct accurately without volume/time.
- RSI has different versions: conventional 14-period Wilder RSI requires adequate prewindow stabilization, but archived individual event-price windows contain only SIX prices before qualifying selloff D0. Thus a reliable D3 RSI14 is NOT computable from canonical source alone. Can calculate independent 5-period Cutler RSI using five consecutive close differences from d3-5..d3; this is a **different, faster oscillator**, label RSI5-CUTLER explicitly. Refrain from treating it as standard RSI14. Avoid interpreting oversold <30 as bottom as it can stay low in trends.
