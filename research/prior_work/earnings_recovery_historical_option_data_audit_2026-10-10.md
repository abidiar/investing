# Historical options data source audit — four frozen 2025 earnings rebound events
Review date: 2026-10-10. User objective: finish the pre-frozen DELL/QCOM/PYPL/AMAT affordable ($50) OTM calls pilot using HISTORICAL market data, not a forward/shadow test. Keep the four events and day3/rising-lows historical entry dates and fixed criteria. No paid data.

## Most promising source: OnclickMedia free historical EOD options backtester
- Docs: https://www.onclickmedia.com/backtest/overview, https://www.onclickmedia.com/backtest/api-docs, https://www.onclickmedia.com/backtest/output-fields
- Provider claims a free (no key) backtest data archive 2013–2026-03-05 spanning 5,000+ tickers, with actual EOD bid/ask observations, option strike, expiration, order-size/volume and EOD bid/ask accounting. The free stand-alone chain API advertises one year of history, so 2025 direct chain API availability may be restricted; the *backtest* historical service claims 2025 is in range. Precise 2025 ticker/date membership unverified.
- Cannot substitute its EOD simulated results for our frozen next-OPEN actual stock trigger! We would use EOD option bid/ask as the best historical approximation and report time mismatch. The backtester selects nearest strike by percentage moneyness and nearest DTE, not necessarily our closest <=$50 contract rule.
- **ACTUAL PROBE COMPLETED** through our Investing OS public GitHub Actions runner: a no-key availability GET to `https://backtest.onclickmedia.com/?ticker={DELL,QCOM,PYPL,AMAT}&list=date` returned HTTP **502 Bad Gateway for ALL four tickers** (Cloudflare origin unavailable/invalid). Therefore no historical EOD quotes fetched. Exact audit stored in `research/earnings_recovery/onclickmedia_four_event_feasibility_2026-10-10.json`. Reproducible script `research/earnings_recovery/run_onclickmedia_four_event_feasibility_2026-10-10.py`, workflow `.github/workflows/earnings-otm-onclick-feasibility.yml`. This is a present provider access problem, not proof no 2025 data exist. Do not claim the test was completed.

## Connected Alpaca — historical options TRADE data
- https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/historical-option-data ; https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/optiontrades ; https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/v1.4.2/reference/optionbars
- Alpaca offers historical options bars and trades from Feb 2024. An Alpaca market-data specialist confirms Basic free users can request OPRA **HISTORICAL BARS/TRADES** >15 minutes old, without requiring real-time OPRA entitlement: https://forum.alpaca.markets/t/data-source-for-option-historical-bars/17704 and https://forum.alpaca.markets/t/paper-trading-options-pricing/17795.
- Crucial: Do NOT claim Alpaca exposes HISTORICAL QUOTE/BID-ASK time series. Its documented public endpoints are option bars, option trades, latest option quotes/snapshots; search of Alpaca API SDK/docs did not reveal a historical-option-quote method. Free *indicative* latest option quotes are MODIFIED, not a real OPRA quote tape. Existing connected Alpaca MCP tools expose only current option chain, contracts, snapshots/latest quotes, NOT get_option_bars/get_option_trades even though official general MCP docs list those methods. No direct credentials are exposed to our tooling. Thus Alpaca is a way to improve second-source historical TIME-STAMPED options trades and fill-option price uncertainty if its historical methods are enabled; **NOT** a verified no-cost NBBO historical solution through our present connector.

## Other sourced providers — excluded for this exact FREE 2025 test
- MarketData.app: https://www.marketdata.app/docs/account/free-accounts/ says Free Forever and even free Trial restricted to 1 year of historical data. The frozen Feb–Aug 2025 entries are >1 year old as of Oct 10, 2026, so not free.
- Alpha Vantage: https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/ HISTORICAL_OPTIONS offers EOD chains back to 2008 but explicitly labeled PREMIUM. Not free for this request.
- Optionistics: https://www.optionistics.com/s/download_historical_data offers per-day historical quotes/Greeks but requires a subscription and data points.
- HistoricalData.net: https://historicaldata.net/options.html provides six months of free SAMPLE options data in Jul–Dec 2022 for TSLA, KO, SPY; doesn't cover our 2025 four names.
- Cboe DataShop options archives require commercial purchase; delayed current quotes are not 2025 historical archives and the Cboe delayed quotes site explicitly prohibits automated extraction.
- Academic/open source 53M options quote-day dataset https://github.com/anahatsingh-ui/options-dataset-hist provides SPY/QQQ/IWM 2008–2025 EOD bid/ask/IV/delta/Greeks, **not** DELL/QCOM/PYPL/AMAT; may support separate calibration, cannot fill our frozen issuer-specific pilot.
- Intrinio EOD API https://docs.intrinio.com/documentation/web_api/get_options_prices_eod_v2 has exactly relevant historical option bid and ask and volume, but requires account/product entitlement and sales-oriented free trial; no verified zero-cost live retrieval for our symbols.
