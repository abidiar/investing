## Existing peer-reviewed evidence
- Shivakumar (2006), *Accruals, Cash Flows and the Post-Earnings-Announcement Drift*, Journal of Business Finance & Accounting https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-5957.2006.01425.x : unexpected cash flows contain information above earnings surprises; distinguishes cash vs accrual news. Our archived pre-report TTM FCF/net-income is a coarse QUALITY proxy, **NOT unexpected announcement-period cash flows**, so cannot claim to replicate this study.
- Chen, Lobo & Zhang (2017), *Accounting Quality, Liquidity Risk and Post-Earnings-Announcement Drift*, Contemporary Accounting Research https://doi.org/10.1111/1911-3846.12310 : accounting quality and liquidity risk interrelate with PEAD; this study's balance sheet proxies do not directly measure liquidity risk or order flow.
- Fink (2021), *A review of the Post-Earnings-Announcement Drift* https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbef.2020.100446 : classic direction-of-news continuation and mechanism survey; 'oversold' != mispricing.
- Chordia et al. (2009), *Liquidity and the Post-Earnings-Announcement Drift* https://business.columbia.edu/faculty/research/liquidity-and-post-earnings-announcement-drift : estimated 70%-100% of naive published drift trading profits offset by costs in illiquid segment; options amplify spread and expiry concerns.
- Yi et al. research on peer information transfers https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1057521917300212 : industry-relative news can be relevant; sector information is not preserved consistently in present 164 event panel.
- Previous internal audit `recovery_winner_loser_forward_eps_source_feasibility_findings_2026-10-11.md`: 91 AH D3 observable PRICE features did not clearly separate 38 D4+3%-first vs 16 stops; historical analyst EPS consensus revisions unavailable from free connected tools, and management guidance proxy 27/72 classifiable FAILED under D3 ±5% entry. Do not relabel these proxies.

Other missingness: forward analyst FY1 EPS revisions, PEG, 2023 industry panel, sector as-of, day4 actual option NBBO, liquidity spread. These are UNAVAILABLE and not zero. No use of earnings release or *postannouncement* 10-Q data as a pre-event factor. Source limitations include current survivor tickers, publication timing, market year clustering and prior study outcomes already inspected.

## Required independent comparators / untested gap
Existing studies have ALREADY tested single price triggers and management forward-guidance proxies without validated benefit. No saved test directly **paired objectively comparable WIN and LOSS events on continuous preannounced annual revenue growth, debt/equity and trailing cash-earnings conversion** with strict calendar + release-time + earnings-reaction magnitude matching, while keeping BOTH 20-session D4 trade outcome and 60-session full preearnings close recovery separate.

The new comparison remains *RETROSPECTIVE DISCOVERY*; not an actual forward walk-forward test. Keep no-future features, match greedily from outcomes **only to balance confounders not to tune labels**, do not scan cutoffs, and report full cohort frequencies and matched subset coverage. Primary scientific verdict NOT SUPPORTED if paired fundamental signs are small/inconsistent, too few matches, or no unconditional utility; even seemingly large paired effects require another independent company/time validation.
