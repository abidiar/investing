## Conflicts and methodological correction
- Support breaks can reveal weak sellers (**mean reversion**) or initiate stop-driven continuation; single confirmation patterns need independent price evidence.
- Earlier synthetic fills/next-bar opens may be optimistic due to spreads, halts, bad ticks, gaps, adverse intra-M5 excursions.
- Daily data may reflect *later* cumulative dividend/split factors; intraday source as-traded. 40-day breakout and support distance membership can change around ex-dates unless the entire decision window is on one basis. Use point-in-time as-traded daily data from M5 RTH aggregation or verify exact corporate-action factors; do NOT merely multiply all bars by one fixed conversion factor across a range that crosses ex-dates.
- Freeze *issuer universe and calendar range* before scanning, and retain every qualifying signal, including all losses, no M5 entries, source unknowns; no excluding ticker merely because it contributes negative result. Same ticker repeated clustering and control M5 timestamp matters. Scans of current index constituents can suffer survivorship; label and disclose.

## What not to retest or optimize
Do not select a new 1/2/3 VWAP-bar rule, shrink 2% stops, change two-session expiry, widen support tolerance or volume after examining old winners; don't use hindsight options volume, no paid quotes, do not infer 63 cases represent a marketwide universe.
