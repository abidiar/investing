# Pre-test literature: failed-low, VWAP-reclaim and volume confirmation after earnings selloff
Date 2026-10-11. Question: For quality issuer >=5% after-hours earnings selloff, after a 3-day post-gap retracement period, does the FIRST intraday 5m failed breakdown followed by session VWAP reclaim and participation on day D4 provide an early entry that beats a non-signal baseline with real observed prices? Before deciding thresholds, reviewed academic, broker/quant published tests, and prior Investing OS research.

## Peer-reviewed / academic
- Miwa (2019), "Short-Term Return Reversals and Intraday Transactions", *Quarterly Journal of Finance* https://doi.org/10.1142/S2010139219500022 . Reversals over next week originated particularly from past INTRADAY negative movement (not overnight return); larger for illiquid stocks and volatile periods, weaker attribution to fundamental news. Does NOT test buying a D4 VWAP reclaim, nor cheap OTM calls.
- Kang, Lin, Xiong (2022), "What drives intraday reversal? Illiquidity or liquidity oversupply?", *Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control* https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jedc.2022.104313 . Liquidity pressure and inventory model can explain US reversal, market-specific microstructure alternative in China. No D4 earnings rule.
- Zhang et al (2024), "Asymmetric post earnings announcement drift and order flow imbalance: The impact on stock market returns", *International Review of Financial Analysis* https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1057521924002485 . Event order flow imbalance contributes to negative-news continuation and bid-ask biases, but aggregate 5m candle volume does NOT reveal buyer-initiated vs seller-initiated trade identity.
- Brogaard, Han, Kim (last revised 2026-10-02), *Intraday Price Pressures* working paper https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4731947 . Across S&P500 30m stock-specific residuals, transitory return reversal strategy more profitable on risk-adjusted metrics than raw-return reversal, over long history; preprint not verified executed call strategy and presumed transaction costs/tradability must be checked. Supports isolating IDIOSYNCRATIC sell pressure, not mechanically VWAP.
- Berkowitz, Logue, Noser (1988), "The Total Cost of Transactions on the NYSE", *Journal of Finance* https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1988.tb02591.x : VWAP used as an EXECUTION/COST BENCHMARK, not established causal predictive institutional-buy signal. Exact historical VWAP proxy based 5m candle HLC3 times reported total volume is approximate, not tick VWAP.
- Akyildirim et al (2021) orderbook imbalance predictability in Borsa Istanbul, *Journal of Multinational Financial Management* https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mulfin.2021.100717 . True buyer/seller initiated flow estimates near-term one minute returns, but 5m total volume is neither trade-sign nor limit book order imbalance, and transfer from Turkey to US AH earnings unclear.

## Independent practitioner / reproducible
- Chat-Backtest (Aug 5 2026) "VWAP reversion intraday: the backtest that lost" https://chatbacktest.com/blog/vwap-strategy-backtest tested RSI3 extreme & 15m VWAP return on SPY/QQQ, found NEGATIVE. Contradicts blanket VWAP mean reversion edge; our signal is failed low + reclaim, not same strategy. Commercial vendor self-reported result, less authoritative than peer-reviewed data.
- Guanalyser study 005 (retrieved Oct2026) https://guanalyser.com/test-bench/opening-range-breakout/ 15-min breakout on 149 large US stocks Apr-Sep2026 9417 trades mean -0.013% gross and -0.053% at 4bps assumed cost; opening range breakout != fail-low VWAP, illustrates multiple pathways to positive-looking chart and execution gap.
- TrueTrader Aug18 2026 self-reported large US 5min opening-range study https://truetrader.net/opening-range-breakout/ 142348 trades, profit factor 1.03 before detailed fill adjustments, reports realized fill erases some edge. Different ORB not exact stock selloff test. Independently unverified.
- github.com/9600dev/mmr `strategies/gap_reversion.py` implements gap fade to VWAP (https://github.com/9600dev/mmr/blob/master/strategies/gap_reversion.py) as code, not evidence of positive expectancy after fills.

## Existing INTERNAL tests and DO-NOT-RETEST
- Daily late D5..20 SMA5 price crossing, volume-naive, plus 3-day relative strength / rising lows (164), FAILED company-disjoint promotion. D3 RSI5/MA5 (164) did not discriminate D4 stock +3-before-5 success in matched samples. The new hypothesis is an actual *first five-minute recovery attempt* and buyer volume after a FAILED intraday breakdown, on D4, BEFORE most move traveled.
- Sector original-company-specific shock variable across164 case-controls suggestive for eventual recovery but weaker in company-disjoint and 2026, not a validated early option filter. Should not require this variable as extra screen in our test.
