## Core economic hypothesis
Stock prices reflect present value of expected future cash flows relative to the discount rate, share count, and market demand/supply. An earnings miss may not destroy **durable earnings power** in a high-profitability, economically entrenched company; investors may re-rate a temporarily weaker margin quarter if longer-term cashflows, orders and market share remain resilient. But an established stock can remain overvalued or experience persistent secular decline despite profitability.

Asness/Frazzini/Pedersen (2019), `Quality Minus Junk`, Review of Accounting Studies, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11142-018-9470-2 empirically establishes profitability+growth+SAFETY persistent quality across markets, risk-adjusted QUALITY factor premiums. Novy-Marx (2013), `The other side of value: The gross profitability premium`, J Financial Economics, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2013.01.003 gross profits/assets predicts cross-sectional equity returns controlling price. **Neither proves initial postearnings gap reversal or D4/5 calls.** Earlier Investing OS on 72 issuer-disjoint events showed standard old quarter revenue YOY growth, debt/equity, TTM FCF/net income were NOT robust D4 or D60 winner discrimination; already SCREENED positive quality (range restriction).

## Institutional fund capital can be actual PRICE PRESSURE, not a price-guarantee
- US Retirement Assets ICI 2026 Q2 release https://www.ici.org/statistical-report/ret_26_q2 : $10.8 TRILLION 401k plan assets as of June 30, 2026, $6.2T held via mutual funds; $3.7T equity funds and $1.7T hybrid including target-date. Broad funds have large capacity for regular payroll investing, but *net* equity inflow can be negative and contributions mostly diversify in index-like proportions. There is NO "buy back to previous individual-company price" trading rule.
- S&P Dow Jones official float-cap-weight index methodology https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/indices/equity/sp-500/ and explanation https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/research-insights/index-literacy/the-sp-500-and-the-dow/ : in cap-weighted fund price falls automatically reduce index constituent weight. Stock PRICE depreciation alone normally does NOT force fund to repurchase extra shares to restore the prior index weight, so passive ownership does NOT create a hard price floor. Net contributions can support broad market prices, but do not prove DELL-specific AH/D4 recovery.
- Coval & Stafford (2007) `Asset fire sales (and purchases) in equity markets`, *J Financial Economics* https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2006.09.007 documented mutual-fund flow-induced temporary displacement from fundamentals with later abnormal return to buyers providing liquidity. Mechanism often months, and no direct evidence that 401ks bought Dell within D4.
- Gabaix & Koijen (2021 NBER) `In Search of the Origins of Financial Fluctuations: The Inelastic Markets Hypothesis` https://www.nber.org/papers/w28967 shows aggregate flows can amplify aggregate price moves; not single-stock support guarantee. Estimates model-specific and not to be transferred as static impact to one issuer.
- SEC 13F holdings known AFTER quarterly reporting, up to 45d lag per SEC https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/division-investment-management-frequently-asked-questions/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-13f : holdings AS OF last quarter disclosed by D3 may show stock INST ownership, but can't tell who purchased stock during D1–D3. Need filing acceptance timestamp.
- Open-market corporate buybacks (instead of retirement contributions) are more direct stock-specific buyers. SEC filing disclosed cap-return and unspent program value by original publication; later quarterly reports reveal subsequent actual repurchase volume, but that later info cannot be used for day4 signal. Meta Q1 2024 repurchased ~$14.64B, Form10Q https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000132680124000049/meta-20240331.htm. SEC Rule 10b-18 safe harbor https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/trading-markets-frequently-asked-questions/division-trading-markets-answers-frequently-asked-questions-concerning-rule-10b-18-safe-harbor; buyback activity can be constrained by company policies, disclosure, MNPI.
- Academic `Is price support a motive for increasing share repurchases?` Journal of Corporate Finance DOI from source https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0929119916300438 documented relationship between active stock repurchases and subsequent price performance, not D4 call edge.

## The sharper investable question
"What distinguishes one earnings miss from another at THE SAME trusted issuer? Is stock down disproportionately relative to immediately knowable changes in next-year earnings power AND forward valuation, while the issuer has a preexisting demonstrated share buyback program or large stable structural holders, and at least temporary selling pressure becomes less severe?"
Not 'profitable company' alone and not 'owned by 401ks' alone. Candidate dislocation vs impairment:
A. **Quality preannounced**: 3yr gross profit ROIC stability; recurring demand/customer retention, moat, market share, sustainable after-capex free cash flow; significant real earnings, manageable net leverage. Include all quality and no per-event survivors rule.
B. **Future expectations change** at announcement and by D3: same FY period guide old vs new and next year analyst revisions (if observed, otherwise unknown), bookings backlog margins/operating guidance, multi-year capex versus incremental returns; analyst snapshot timing strict.
C. **Valuation**: old vs D3 price / preannounced forward earnings or normalized free cashflow, post-earnings implied earnings yield not simply historic P/E; negative market reaction vs magnitude of actual FY EPS downgrade and stock+industry relative shock. Historical EPS revisions source unavailable free currently, future new prospective snapshots supported if provider data meets as-of.
D. **Sustainable buyback support**: prior 2–4 quarters ACTUAL repurchases as fraction of float and average daily volume, cash flow-funded, nonexpired authorization remaining, net share count after dilution, as-of publication; flag repurchase authorization vs confirmed buying. Buyback can stop/expire and is no guaranteed bid.
E. **Passive/institutional demand**: index membership AT EVENT DAY, freely tradeable float, 13F public quarter BEFORE entry, concentration in funds with known inflows, contemporaneous broad net equity fund flows if as-of source available. 401k actual stock flow is not directly observed; don't label fund ownership as buying.
F. **Opportunity horizon**: D4 low/target-first vs long-run D60 full reclaim, and real dollar-priced executable calls with theta/IV (cheap OTM far too fragile to use eventual stock reclaim alone).
