**Frozen 2026-10-11 before retrieving 2019-2021 stock/benchmark D1/D2 event outcomes.** Only generic 2018-2021 AAPL/MSFT daily-feed availability and SPY 2019-03-06 M5 coverage were checked; neither was inspected for strategy returns. Previous 2022 established support results were viewed. Do not silently change entry rule, thresholds, universe, or stopping period.

## Core hypothesis

Market-wide recovery is observable as of entry: a **Grimes-inspired stock support failed breakdown** accompanied by **SPY rising since that morning's 09:30 open** has a higher conditional target-first frequency and higher *net expected return per trade* than the *same stock failure pattern without SPY morning recovery*. This is a **market-price proxy**, NOT the proprietary Hanna CBI, the TICK oscillator, or a broad NYSE advancers/decliners measurement. A separate alternative hypothesis about genuine higher-timeframe bull-flag patterns remains untested.

## Five-minute trade and market overlay

- For each frozen D0: require source D0, D1, D2 stock regular-session five-minute 78-bar histories; use SPY D1 78-bar M5 and as-of entry price from these. Missing any full required bars, source-date discrepancy, or adjusted D0 OHLC versus M5 rebase mismatch >3% => UNKNOWN, not wins/failures/skips. Map each bar by 09:30 Eastern exchange session, taking daylight saving into account.
- Stock support breaching failure pattern exactly prior 2022 protocol: first D1 five-minute bar starting 09:30-11:45 ET with low **strictly below 0.999 * support**; **same or immediately following** five-minute candle close >= support. Confirmation complete by 11:50; buy following M5 bar OPEN <= noon. First match only. Entry <=1.02*support; structural stop 0.999*min(breach-bar low, confirmation-bar low), structural distance 0.35%-2%; target +2.00% from entry; D1 and D2 RTH monitoring; opening gaps filled at actual open, otherwise stop-first on ambiguous bar; D2 15:55 exit timeout; model 0.50% roundtrip stock-equivalent costs. Skips contribute 0 to per-evaluable-signal mean.
- **Pre-specified single SPY context gate, no tuning:** At the stock entry timestamp, use the **most recent fully completed SPY M5 candle close** (bar at index stockEntryIndex-1); subtract SPY D1 initial 09:30 candle OPEN. `MARKET_RECOVERING` iff previous completed SPY close > D1 initial SPY open (strictly positive open-to-now intraday move). All others `MARKET_NOT_RECOVERING`. SPY must have a complete RTH M5 D1 session. Classification is only applied at objectively determined stock entry time (not at D0); no SPY D1 future bars leak. Do not shift entry to wait for SPY gate.
- Evaluate **the exact same stock fills and stock exits** in conditional SPY_RECOVERING and NOT_RECOVERING groups. The only change is selecting/abstaining on actual eligible fills. Do not pretend all candidate days had a recovery signal. Compare sign-defined conditionals with **unfiltered price-only trades**, next-open +2/-2 stock benchmark per full evaluable daily signal, and net return per event including SPY gate skips. A zero-trade subset means UNKNOWN EXPECTANCY; no claims.
- **User-preferred 10:30-noon** is *predeclared subset*, entryIndex>=12 (09:30 index0). Report performance both entire before-noon fills and this subset without moving morning entry times and without filtering until outputs are revealed. No time-window optimization, no post hoc quartile threshold search.
- Output 2019/2020/2021 separate performance and 2020 COVID crash sensitivity; clustered 10,000-replicate bootstrap by ticker and by D0 date (seed 20261011), when adequate sample size, and explicit zero/very-wide intervals. Include SPY gate effect as *net return difference among filled trades* and conservative confidence intervals; market-gated per-signal mean includes 0 for abstentions. Preserve source Webull M5 and per-event data.
- **No free option NBBO** in this test: +2% stock target and modeled 50bp costs are not call-option P&L, nor verification of sub-$50 option fills.
- Do not claim replication of Hanna's proprietary breadth signals or Adam Grimes' hand-selected context-sensitive bull flag failure; this is one independently frozen mechanical hypothesis. No scanner/automation changes absent separately validated repeatable edge.
