2. 3. Chen/Cohen/Liang/Sun, *Maxing out short-term reversals in weekly stock returns*, Journal of Empirical Finance 2025 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jempfin.2025.101608): high-recent-MAX weekly loser/winner portfolios ~1.66% weekly vs ~0.65% low-MAX; concentrated in smaller, illiquid stocks with high retail order imbalance, NOT a simple high-MAX long CALL system and institutional bid/ask costs matter.
4. Zhang/Gregoriou/Wu, *Asymmetric post earnings announcement drift and order flow imbalance*, International Review of Financial Analysis 2024 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.irfa.2024.103316), 17,304 FTSE350 firm quarters 2000-2021, direct quote/trade order flow supports conditional mechanism but not exact U.S. M5 bar-volume proxy.
