## Rob Hanna — what was actually studied

### NYSE closing TICK TomOscillator
1. Original formula publicly described May 13, 2011 by Hanna, crediting Tom McClellan: x_t=NYSE end-of-day closing TICK; a_t=SMA_10(x); b_t=SMA_10(a); oscillator=a_t-b_t. Then 1-year (approximately trailing 252 session) percentile rank of oscillator at date t, to normalize evolving NYSE population/short sale rule. Source original https://quantifiableedges.blogspot.com/2011/05/how-nyse-closing-tick-can-be-utilized.html and updated reprint https://quantifiableedges.com/blog/ . This is NOT ordinary stock up/down volume or intraday candle volume. No original-day TICK close history verified from currently connected Webull US_STOCK/US_ETF daily symbols; must NOT fake it via SPY OHLC or one ETF.
2. June 7 2026 original post archived at https://quantifiableedges.com/blog/ heading "A TICK TomOscillator Study Suggesting a Monday Bounce": Friday June 5 oscillator percentile <1st of preceding year; Hanna historical 23/25 S&P500 closed HIGHER NEXT DAY versus previous close. It is a next-close **DIRECTION** outcome, not next-open-to-close SPY return or option PnL. No report of execution spread, drawdown, or options. Friday signal observed as-of close, actionable next session.
3. **Prospective sanity check after publication** Monday June 8 2026: actual SPX June5 close 7,383.74, June8 open 7,440.57 and close 7,405.73. June8 close-close +0.30%, satisfies close-up; next-session open->close approximately -0.468%, so a Monday-opening underlying long held to close LOST before trading costs, while published indicator "hit". Verified https://www.statmuse.com/money/ask/sandp-500-chart-for-june-2026 ; AP https://apnews.com/article/bdfcaff60df7b2a2bd60b51ecf85d0ff and https://www.reuters.com/business/sp-500-nasdaq-futures-climb-chip-stocks-stabilize-2026-06-08/ . Need measure actual entry-to-exit on price and call premiums rather than quote 92% as options edge.
4. Actual oscillator replication possible ONLY when original dated historical closing NYSE TICK available, plus precise 252 prior valid observations and exact percentile rank. Otherwise mark unavailable. Need evaluate base-rate close-to-close, open-to-close, first +0.5/1%-before downside, worst adverse excursion, cost/IV and independent distinct crises cluster CI.

### Capitulative Breadth Indicator (CBI), distinctly DIFFERENT
1. Hanna's CBI = number of active proprietary *Catapult system* trade signals within S&P100, **NOT** a count of oversold stock RSI or generic number of S&P stocks <moving average. Formula for Catapult is not disclosed fully; self-described in https://quantifiableedges.com/using-qe-to-your-advantage-subscriber-tools-1-the-catapult-the-cbi/ . Original January6 2008 source https://quantifiableedges.com/my-capitulative-breadth-indicator/ : levels 7 and 10, buy SPX when high CBI, exit when CBI <=3, may precede lows, scale-in discretionary. Exact history requires original historical constituents plus original Catapult time series; free original research paper from 2018 may be available with **free QE login**, https://quantifiableedges.com/intraducing-the-quantifiable-edges-cbi-research-paper/ but does not imply underlying daily series is freely downloadable.
2. Jan13 2025 refresh https://quantifiableedges.com/cbi-hits-10-a-study/ : CBI >=10 with exit <=3, 2020 pandemic accounts for most gross strategy losses despite 9 most recent instances positive. Historic performance is index and variable holding period, not 1–2-day calls.
3. Feb6 2018 CBI >=25 extremely rare, 5 historic instances, all SPX bounced in next5d, BUT **3/5 first fell further 4–8.5%**. https://quantifiableedges.com/when-the-quantifiable-edges-capitulative-breadth-indicator-hits-25/ . Fatal for far OTM $50 small-account short dated calls. Signals measure **background capitulation probability**, not a safe execution moment.
4. Hanna 2024 31-page SSRN research (NAAIM first prize) https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4808230 (abstract): past SPX price and oversold indicators predicted subsequent VIX futures movement more usefully than VIX alone predicted SPX. This supports separating SPX price regime from VIX option prices; NOT a profit guarantee or index +2/−1 next session.

## Adam Grimes — actual failure test pattern and quantifiability
1. Primary https://www.adamhgrimes.com/failure-test-2/ (Nov11 2014), https://www.adamhgrimes.com/trading-pattern-the-failure-test/ (Jul24 2012). Failure test / Wyckoff Spring (below support) or Upthrust (above resistance): known as-of previous significant pivot zone, price breaches it then closes back inside SAME bar, or breaching bar followed by quick recovery NEXT bar; **long** can enter on reversal bar close, with stop BELOW breaching-bar low. Short symmetrical. Favors range edge or overextended trend extreme; price structure and next-bar follow-through important. Overnight gaps can invalidate stop and cause losses larger than expected; discretionary position sizing smaller. He gives case examples, not a public 80% quantified trade success series with original individual event dates and trade fills.
2. **VITAL CORRECTION TO OUR PREVIOUS V4 PLAN:** his volume Q&A original https://www.adamhgrimes.com/reader-questions-volume-and-supportresistance/ July26 2012 expressly says he COULD NOT VERIFY trading volume's incremental usefulness to the failure-test pattern, proposes >=200 examples and separate WITH vs WITHOUT volume cue for an objective test. Consequently, our v4 M5 "positive volume >=60% on prior6 bars" plus higher-low and 10am window are **OURS**, not Grimes's published trigger, and can delay/miss the original close/reclaim entry. Do not attribute additional volume+two rising bars as a validation of Grimes. Run PRICE-ONLY failure test vs independently OPTIONAL volume-proxy filter and directly score incremental lift.
3. Grimes context and gap risks https://www.adamhgrimes.com/chart-of-the-day-failure-test-in-aapl-2/ . Volatility study published Oct5 2026 https://www.adamhgrimes.com/volatility-shocks-and-what-follows/ : after ES day TR>2.5× ATR20, next 5-session mean daily TR ~1.71× current ATR20 (shock included in ATR), but 70/79 future five-day average TR below initial shock-day daily range. Dataset ES 2010 through Sep2026, overlapping future windows and stock market index/ES not all small names. Supports potential EXPENSIVE IV + still-large underlying gap risk; not direction.
4. Same Grimes author distinguishes a quick rejection bar and entry on close/next; arbitrary two-VWAP-closes rule had previously failed in distinct 49-issuer 2024–26 as-archived M5 sample, and is not synonymous with original Grimes.
