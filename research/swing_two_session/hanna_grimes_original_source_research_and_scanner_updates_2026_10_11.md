## ROB HANNA original research (not reducible to RSI oversold)
### A. Closing NYSE TICK TomOscillator, reproducible if genuine series
**2011 PRIMARY FORMULA** https://quantifiableedges.blogspot.com/2011/05/how-nyse-closing-tick-can-be-utilized.html :
Closing TICK is # NYSE issues trading last on an uptick minus # on a downtick AT THE CLOSING READING. Let `MA10 = simple mean of last 10 closing TICK readings`, `MA10_of_MA10 = 10-session SMA of the initial MA10`; oscillator = MA10 - MA10_of_MA10 (verify moving average specific alignment). Hanna applied rolling **1-year percent-rank** (about 252 trading days) to control changing number of NYSE listings and regulatory changes. Historical TICK readings MUST be source-confirmed; Webull per-stock candle bars do NOT imply this series, even when technically able to fetch stock prices.
June07 2026 report https://quantifiableedges.com/a-tick-tomoscillator-study-suggesting-a-monday-bounce/ : **TomOscillator 1-year percentile under 1%** on Friday June05, **23 of 25** such historical cases had **next session positive close** in SPX; one-day DIRECTION of index, not +2% before -1% on an individual stock and not profit for calls. Single rare sample, vintage, overlapping events, full per-event years, entry/exit timing and trading fees NOT released in the textual article. 3pm ET scanner CANNOT use SAME-DAY closing TICK before ~4pm; latest completed prior session only. If not available mark UNKNOWN, not "neutral"/"bullish".
Older 2013 application https://quantifiableedges.com/the-tick-tomoscillator-how-it-is-suggesting-a-bounce/ used oscillator raw under -200 PLUS 5d SPX low PLUS index above 200 SMA; different spec from 2026 percentile. Do not mix without separately frozen tests. For 2026 <1% test, independent 2024–26 has only a few occurrences; needs historical source and robust calendar vintage audit.

## ADAM GRIMES original research
### A. The failure test / 2B / Wyckoff spring is an EX-ANTE price pattern, not simply VWAP
Original https://www.adamhgrimes.com/trading-pattern-the-failure-test/ (2012), https://www.adamhgrimes.com/failure-test-2/ (2014) and book chapter https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/9781119202837.ch6 .
Take a pivot BEFORE the signal; stock briefly trades *below* prior legitimate support; fails promptly to sustain the breakdown and **completes same or next bar back above**, entry at reclaim bar CLOSE or next bar OPEN; stop beyond probe extreme, never choose the pivot after seeing the bounce. Direction reversal at old resistance mirrors for short. He explicitly warns not all patterns win and GAPS can exceed stop. His original simple strategy did not require a two-close VWAP pattern, nor a subsequent delayed higher low: our added higher-low volume-condition is our *separate stricter candidate variant* and requires pre-registered testing; do not attribute exact proxy rules to Grimes.
Differentiation from existing 63/103 old data: trade STRUCTURAL FAILED TEST of an authentic prior support and entry immediately after reclaim; not buy after next-day two VWAP closes, not long simply because down7%. For user $50 calls, require that 2R profit runway is still available from the next bar opening price before early option decay.

### B. 2019 test: authentic market price levels vs shifted controls
Original https://www.adamhgrimes.com/when-is-a-human-better-than-a-machine-using-human-discretion-in-quantitative-testing/ , real previous-day/overnight ES levels vs shifted +/−~20% ATR fake levels; users distinguish genuine better than shifted in ES, but no effect in EURUSD round-number test. Not proof a company daily 40-day range low predicts +2 before -1, but suggests randomized/shifted-pivot CONTROL should be included in future pattern tests. Same-sector/ATR matched peers alone not sufficient for testing level validity.

### C. 2026 ES futures vol research & options premium
Primary research Oct05 https://www.adamhgrimes.com/volatility-shocks-and-what-follows/ : ES daily futures 2010-Sep2026, when an ES true-range shock >2.5× ATR20, mean true range next five trading sessions **1.71× ATR20** but **0.60× shock bar TR**; in **70 of 79** events future average smaller than shock day's extreme. Historical index future VOLATILITY, not evidence of bullish DIRECTION or of options profit. Down closing ES adds ~0.20 ATR20 to expected next day range vs an up close, ~0.13 ATR over following five-session mean, less strong in individual stocks. His model lowered log RMSE realized-vol forecasts 0.366 to0.303. An option can LOSE with correct directional forecast when IV collapses below premium implied assumptions; compare CURRENT call implied with REALISTIC prospective realized movement/term structure, wide spread and DTE. **Do NOT apply 70/79 as 89% trade wins**.
Low volatility NR7 futures episodes also tend to continue quiet, not explode, warning against naive compression-breakout logic.
October10 breadth myth https://www.adamhgrimes.com/the-breadth-myth-what-market-data-really-says/ — poor breadth does not mechanically predict imminent crash; no predictive scoring points automatically awarded for divergences.
